# Basic Savings Account

## What is the Basic Savings Account
The Basic Savings Account is a simple everyday account for salary, savings and small payments. You get a debit card, mobile and internet banking, and account alerts by SMS and email. This is illustrative demo content for Sample Bank; replace it with your bank's approved product notes.

## What are the fees and interest rates
This assistant cannot quote current interest rates, charges or minimum balances. Please check the official rate card and fee schedule on the bank's website or ask a bank advisor. Nothing here is financial advice.

## Who can apply
Any resident adult (18 or older) who can complete identity and address verification can apply online. The application is completed in this chat, and you can stop and return later; your progress is saved.
