# Your details

## Why do you need my mobile number and email
Your mobile number and email are used to send one-time passwords, account alerts and important notices about your application. We never share them with other customers.

## Why do you ask about my occupation
Banks record occupation to understand how an account is likely to be used and to meet regulatory requirements. Choose the option that fits you best.

## Why do you need my name, date of birth and address
These are read from your documents and compared across them to check that they match. If they differ, you will be asked which one is correct.

## Can I save my progress and come back later
Yes. Your progress is saved after every step. You can close the browser and return later to continue where you stopped.
