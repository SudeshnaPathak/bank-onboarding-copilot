# What happens next

## What happens after I submit
When you submit, your application goes to a bank reviewer along with a summary of what the assistant found. You will see the status in this chat.

## What if more information is needed
The reviewer may ask for more information, for example a clearer photo or a corrected detail. You will get a message here and can upload or update it, then submit again.

## What happens after approval
Once your account is approved, you will be guided through the first steps such as making your first deposit and activating your card. Timelines depend on the bank and are not guaranteed by this assistant.
