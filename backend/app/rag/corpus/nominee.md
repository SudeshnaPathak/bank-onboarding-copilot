# Nominee

## What is a nominee
A nominee is the person you choose to receive the money in your account if something happens to you. Naming a nominee makes it much simpler for your family to claim the balance.

## Do I have to add a nominee
Adding a nominee is optional during this application. You can skip it now and add one later through the bank.

## What details do you ask for the nominee
We ask for the nominee's name and their relationship to you, such as spouse, parent, child or sibling.
