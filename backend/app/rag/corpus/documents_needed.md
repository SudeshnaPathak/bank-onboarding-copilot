# Documents needed

## What documents do I need
For the Basic Savings Account you need three documents: your PAN card, your Aadhaar card and your driving licence. Keep the originals with you and take clear photos of each.

## Why is each document needed
The PAN card is your tax identity. The Aadhaar card confirms your identity and address. The driving licence is an additional proof of address and date of birth, and it shows whether the document is still valid.

## How do I take a good photo
Place the card on a flat, plain surface in good light. Make sure all four corners are visible and the text is sharp. Avoid glare, shadows and blur. If a photo is unclear, this assistant will ask you to retake it straight away. JPG, PNG and PDF files are accepted.

## What if my documents show different details
It is common for an address or a name spelling to differ between documents. The assistant will show you what it read and ask which is correct. A human reviewer checks every application.
