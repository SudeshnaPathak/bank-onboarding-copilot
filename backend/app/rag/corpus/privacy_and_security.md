# Privacy and security

## Is my data safe and how are my documents protected
Your document photos are stored encrypted. Identity numbers are masked inside the chat, showing only the last few characters. Only authorised bank staff can view your documents.

## Who makes the decision
An AI assistant helps read your documents, points out differences and prepares a summary. It never approves or rejects an application. A human reviewer always makes the final decision.

## What will the assistant not do
The assistant does not give investment or financial advice, and it cannot predict whether an application will be approved. For those questions, please speak with a bank advisor.
