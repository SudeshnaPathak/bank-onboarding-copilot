# KYC explained

## What is KYC
KYC stands for Know Your Customer. It is the process banks use to confirm who you are and where you live before opening an account. It helps protect you and the bank from identity theft, fraud and money laundering.

## Why does the bank ask for documents
Your identity documents let the bank check your name, date of birth and address against official records. We read the details from your photos so you do not have to type them, and we ask you to confirm anything that looks unclear or differs between documents.

## Is KYC a one-time thing
Banks may ask you to refresh your KYC details from time to time. If your address or name changes, tell the bank so records stay accurate.
