# FATCA declaration

## What is FATCA
FATCA is a United States tax law. Banks around the world must ask customers whether they are tax residents of the United States, and report certain accounts of US tax residents.

## Why do you ask me this
We ask one simple question: are you a tax resident of the United States? Most customers answer no. Your answer is recorded with your application and reviewed by a person.

## What if I am not sure
If you are not sure about your tax residency, please speak with a tax advisor or a bank advisor before answering.
